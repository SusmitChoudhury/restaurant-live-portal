### 3. Key Operational Benefits for Your Restaurant

#### A. Increase Average Order Value by 15% to 22%
- **Visual Food Photography**: Every dish features mouth-watering, high-resolution imagery. Visual menus statistically drive higher appetites and premium dish selections compared to plain paper text menus.
- **Automated Add-on Upsells**: Guests are prompted with culinary extras (Artisanal Cheese, Truffle Dips, Extra Sauces) in the cart, consistently adding ₹100–₹300 per order with zero staff effort.

#### B. Eliminate Order Errors & Allergy Liabilities
- **Customer Special Requests**: Guests enter precise instructions ("Mild spice", "Peanut allergy", "Extra crispy") directly into the **Note for Chef** box.
- **Direct Kitchen Dispatch**: Notes appear in a bold amber callout box directly on the kitchen ticket, eliminating transcription errors from hurried waitstaff.

#### C. Eliminate Out-of-Stock Embarrassment
- With traditional paper menus, a customer orders a signature dish, only for the waiter to return 10 minutes later with apologies that the kitchen is out of ingredients.
- With Aura Royale, the head chef taps **"Out of Stock"** on the kitchen screen, and the dish instantly grays out across all guest phones on the dining floor.

#### D. Accelerate Table Turnover by 15–20 Minutes
- Guests sit down, scan the table QR code, browse, and place their initial drink and appetizer orders within 2 minutes of seating.
- Subsequent re-orders (main courses, desserts, drinks) can be dispatched with 1 tap, reducing the time guests spend waiting for servers to notice them.

### 4. Zero Additional Hardware Required

The Aura Royale system is 100% web-based and runs on the hardware your restaurant already owns:
- **For Dining Guests**: Any modern smartphone (Apple iPhone, Android) via camera QR scan. No app download or registration required.
- **For Kitchen Staff**: Any existing Android tablet, iPad, kitchen display screen, or staff smartphone.
- **For Managers**: Any laptop, PC, or phone browser with protected password login (`Smile@123`).
